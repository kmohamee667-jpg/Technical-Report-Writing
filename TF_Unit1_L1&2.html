Technical Report Writing

Unit 1: True / False Questions Bank
Q1. Effective communication is about transmitting your message to other people clearly and obviously.
Correct Answer: A. True
Q2. The further away your job is from manual work, the less important it is to know how to convey your thoughts.
Correct Answer: B. False
Q3. Sensing a need is the very first step in the communication process.
Correct Answer: A. True
Q4. In the communication process, "Defining the situation" involves making preliminary choices about the message content and format.
Correct Answer: B. False
Q5. "Considering possible communication strategies" means choosing between different options to solve a situation.
Correct Answer: A. True
Q6. If you decided to present the message orally, you should make notes or write the whole presentation during the "Composing the message" step.
Correct Answer: A. True
Q7. Interpreting the message is an activity done by the sender.
Correct Answer: B. False
Q8. Adapting your message carefully to your recipient increases the chance of achieving the desired response.
Correct Answer: A. True
Q9. Replying to the message is the final step in the communication process where the receiver acts as the communicator.
Correct Answer: A. True
Q10. Writing helps you think by finding logical faults in your own understanding.
Correct Answer: A. True
Q11. Making a list of pros and cons is considered a practical reason for writing.
Correct Answer: B. False
Q12. Applying for a job is considered an intellectual reason for writing.
Correct Answer: B. False
Q13. Writing your CV to reflect your qualification is a practical reason for learning writing skills.
Correct Answer: A. True
Q14. Web writing is always the best way of obtaining information because it contains no mistakes.
Correct Answer: B. False
Q15. Poor writing skills when presenting a business proposal might result in the rejection of the proposal.
Correct Answer: A. True
Q16. Because of technology advancement, the importance of writing is decreasing nowadays.
Correct Answer: B. False
Q17. Different situations may require different communication strategies.
Correct Answer: A. True
Q18. Writing skills are not essential because we can learn and read scientific research papers online.
Correct Answer: B. False
Q19. Internal operational communication includes advertising made to customers.
Correct Answer: B. False
Q20. Orders and instructions given by supervisors are examples of internal operational communication.
Correct Answer: A. True
Q21. Personal communication only occurs in personal life, never in the workplace.
Correct Answer: B. False
Q22. A business message must fulfill a degree of formality in transmission to achieve its goal.
Correct Answer: A. True
Q23. Verbal business messages are frequently passed on to carry out routine business activities.
Correct Answer: A. True
Q24. The written business message is used when the information being passed on needs to be filed and compiled.
Correct Answer: A. True
Q25. A routine business message can be used to commend an employee for a well-done job.
Correct Answer: B. False
Q26. A negative business message aims to convey frustration without being rude, maintaining professionalism.
Correct Answer: A. True
Q27. A sales letter persuading the reader to buy a product is an example of a persuasive business message.
Correct Answer: A. True
