Technical Report Writing

Interactive Question Bank - Unit 1
Q1. In the communication process, which step involves extracting information, guessing the purpose, and making judgments?
Correct Answer: B. Interpreting the message
Q2. Which of the following is considered an "Intellectual reason" for writing?
Correct Answer: B. Create Large Amounts of Information
Q3. Summarizing your reading for future reference is an example of which reason for writing?
Correct Answer: C. Create Large Amounts of Information
Q4. Which practical reason for writing involves filling out different forms and applications?
Correct Answer: C. Government
Q5. When we consider the communication activities of an organization from an overall point of view, the activities fall into three broad categories:
Correct Answer: C. internal operational, external operational, and personal
Q6. External communication can involve communicating with all below except:
Correct Answer: D. Colleagues
Q7. Which type of communication helps in making and sustaining the relationships upon which business depends and affects employees' productivity?
Correct Answer: C. Personal communication
Q8. Which type of business message is considered the primary mode of passing a message to carry out routine business activities?
Correct Answer: B. Verbal Business Message
Q9. Which type of business message is used to convey details about an upcoming event, like a monthly staff meeting date?
Correct Answer: A. Routine Business Message
Q10. Commending a salesman for having reached the month's quota is an example of a:
Correct Answer: C. Positive Business Message
Q11. A delay notification and a financial penalty warning sent to non-punctual employees is an example of a:
Correct Answer: B. Negative Business Message
Q12. There are many types of business message which are:
Correct Answer: D. All of the above.
Q13. The business message could not be:
Correct Answer: B. Conceptual.
